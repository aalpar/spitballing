# Spitball result — premises for a 1.5-hour Daria TV-movie special

**Request:** premises for a 1.5-hour Daria TV-movie special
**Value criterion:** unmistakably Daria (deadpan voice, Lawndale, conformity/media
satire), sustains 90 minutes, not a re-tread of the two existing movies.
**Method:** 12-agent blind panel mapped the obvious set (9 clusters); candidates
sampled in the high-value/non-obvious band; ranked value × fringe
(`0.6·fringe + 0.4·value`).

## The final 5 (band, ranked)

| # | Premise | v | f | score |
|---|---------|---|---|-------|
| 1 | **WrongObit** — the paper accidentally runs Daria's obituary; watching the town react to her "death" tells her exactly how invisible she chose to be. | 0.80 | 0.70 | 0.740 |
| 2 | **Eulogy** — a teacher dies mid-semester and the school stages a grotesque performance of grief; Daria's refusal to perform mourning reads as heartless until she finds the one honest thing to say. | 0.86 | 0.62 | 0.716 |
| 3 | **PlantClose** — Lawndale's defining employer shuts down and the town starts to die; Daria's reflexive mockery curdles into grief for a place she pretended not to belong to. | 0.85 | 0.60 | 0.700 |
| 4 | **Obituary** — Daria's zine gets her hired writing obituaries; writing honestly about strangers' finished lives cracks her certainty that nothing matters. | 0.82 | 0.62 | 0.700 |
| 5 | **Gentrify** — developers rebrand Lawndale a trendy "authentic" suburb and Daria finds her own ironic sensibility has become the marketing language pricing out everyone who made the place real. | 0.75 | 0.66 | 0.696 |

**The band has a shape: mortality and material decline.** Four of the five put
Daria's detachment in front of something it genuinely can't deflect — a death
(WrongObit, Eulogy, Obituary) or a place dying (PlantClose, Gentrify). The naive
panel almost never went there; it reached for *institutions to expose* and *foils
to spar with*, not *losses to survive*.

> **Honest caveat — spread:** #1 (WrongObit) and #4 (Obituary) are near-siblings
> (both "Daria + the obituary desk"). The ranker scores each candidate
> independently and has no de-duplication, so a vein with several strong members
> can occupy multiple slots. Treat them as one idea with two executions; the
> distinct fifth pick beneath them is **DeadEnd** (score 0.696, tied with Gentrify).

## What the panel made obvious (rejected — 9 clusters)

All ≥25% of 12 panelists:

`doc_reality` (9/12) · `cynical_foil` (7) · `wellness_scheme` (7) ·
`college_cynicism` (6) · `jane_artcareer` (5) · `accidentally_cares` (4) ·
`local_politics` (4) · `detachment_cost` (3) · `jane_leaves` (3)

i.e. the film-crew-comes-to-town movie, the more-cynical-foil movie, the
corporate/wellness-scheme-to-expose movie, the college-essay-reveals-her-armor
movie, Jane's-art-career movie, the accidentally-cares movie, the local-politics
movie, the cost-of-detachment movie, and the Jane-leaves movie.

**Sharpest result:** `detachment_cost` — "her irony is commodified / her
detachment has a cost" — landed at exactly 0.25 (3/12). That is the *obvious*
move, not a clever one: a tourism-rebrand-of-her-irony premise (close to
`Gentrify`) is riding a vein three independent panelists already reached. The
band had to push past it into actual loss.

## Negative fringe (the compass the band hugged)

- Daria's voice but no Lawndale / no satire engine
- a generic teen plot with Daria pasted on (prom, makeover, love triangle)
- a genre break (zombie outbreak, musical) — abandons realist satire
- Daria absent / a bystander to a side character's plot
- an extended episode with no movie-scale stakes

The winners sit one step inside that border: real stakes (death, a town's
collapse) handled in Daria's register rather than melodrama's — close enough to
"too sentimental to be Daria" to be non-obvious, disciplined enough to still be
her.
