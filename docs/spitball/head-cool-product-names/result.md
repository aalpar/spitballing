# Spitball result — names for a product that keeps the head cool

**Request:** new names for a product that keeps the head cool
**Value criterion:** a short product name that communicates cooling the head
**Method:** boundary sampling — 12-agent blind panel mapped the obvious set;
candidates sampled in the high-value/non-obvious band; ranked value × fringe
(`0.6·fringe + 0.4·value` — surprise leads, value pulls).

## The final 5 (band, ranked)

| # | Name | value | fringe | score | why it fulfills | why it's not obvious |
|---|------|-------|--------|-------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Oasis** | 0.66 | 0.78 | 0.732 | relief from heat — the felt benefit of a cooled head | no temperature word at all; evokes the *result*, not the mechanism |
| 2 | **Eclipse** | 0.60 | 0.76 | 0.696 | shadow falling over you — cooling by shade | reads as astronomy, not cooling; the cooling is inferred |
| 3 | **Umbra** | 0.68 | 0.70 | 0.692 | the shade's core — direct shade-over-head image | Latin/obscure; no panelist reached for shade |
| 4 | **Aurora** | 0.62 | 0.74 | 0.692 | northern, cold-sky, a halo *over the head* | a light phenomenon first; coldness is connotation |
| 5 | **Penumbra** | 0.60 | 0.72 | 0.672 | partial shade over the head | same shade vein, even less literal |

> Ranking weighting retuned 0.7/0.3 → **0.6/0.4** (fringe still leads, value pulls
> harder). Effect at the top: the same 5 names survive, but higher-value **Umbra**
> (0.68) edges past **Aurora** (0.62) — they tie at 0.692 and value breaks it.

The band leans on **two metaphors the naive panel never touched**: *shade*
(Umbra, Penumbra, Eclipse) and *relief* (Oasis). Every panelist reached instead
for a temperature word + head/cap compound.

## What the panel made obvious (rejected)

All ≥25% of 12 panelists — the positive set, off-limits:

`cool` (12/12) · `frost` (12/12) · `chill` (12/12) · `airflow` (12/12) ·
`ice` (11/12) · `mental`/calm-pun (9/12) · `therma` (8/12)

i.e. *every* literal cold lexeme, plus airflow words and "keep a cool head"
calm puns. The map (`map.json`) holds the member ideas per cluster.

## Negative fringe (the compass the band hugged)

- evokes cold but not the head (Glacier, Frostbite)
- evokes the head but not cooling (Cranium, Noggin)
- figurative "cool head" = composure, not temperature (Poise, Composure)
- evokes comfort, not cooling (Comfy, Cushion)

The winners sit one step inside that border: they evoke the *experience* of a
cooled head without naming temperature — close enough to "doesn't fulfill" to be
non-obvious, far enough to still land.
